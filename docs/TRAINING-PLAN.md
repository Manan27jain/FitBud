**The one rule that matters:** 80% of your weekly running minutes must be Z1–Z2. Every run in your history sits in Z4. Fixing that is the whole plan.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. **Sleep gate** — under 6h, the day's quality session becomes easy Z2. Under 5h, it becomes rest.
2. **Recovery gate** — resting HR +7bpm over your 30-day average, or HRV −25%, for two days running → next session drops to Z1 or rest.
3. **Progression caps** — weekly volume never rises more than 20% over the last *non-cutback* week; the long run never rises more than 2km.
4. **Week 4 cutback is immutable.** So is the taper in weeks 7–8. Feeling good is not a reason to add.
5. **Long run caps at 16km.** Taper and race-day adrenaline cover the last 5km — this is normal for a half.
